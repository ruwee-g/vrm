Animation assets
================

idle_loop.vrma
  Source: github.com/pixiv/ChatVRM (public/idle_loop.vrma)
  License: MIT (see LICENSE_ChatVRM.txt)
  Used as the default looping idle motion.

LookAround.vrma, Relax.vrma
  Source: github.com/tk256ailab/vrm-viewer (VRMA/*.vrma)
  License: MIT (see LICENSE_vrm-viewer.txt)
  Used as occasional idle variations.

VRMA_01.vrma - VRMA_06.vrma
  Source: VRoid Project / pixiv Inc., free motion pack distributed on BOOTH
  (https://vroid.booth.pm/items/5512385). Copies taken from
  github.com/ARPAHLS/avatar (avatar/src/assets/avatars/VRMA/).
  Full terms: Readme_VRMA_MotionPack_EN.txt.
  VRMA_01.vrma - VRMA_06.vrma are bundled as reaction animations; the mapping
  is defined in src/reactions.ts. The pack's squat motion (VRMA_07) is not used.

dance-simple.glb
  Source: Mesh2Motion, static/assets/human/animations/dance-simple.glb
  https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (LICENSE-CC0.MD: all animations are CC0).
  Retargeted to the VRM humanoid in src/danceClip.ts; arm motion is replaced
  with a relaxed down-by-the-side pose and very small arm/hand movement.
  Used as the primary looping dance animation.

dance.vrma
  Source: ekza-space/ekza-studio CC0 starter pack
  (apps/web/public/library/animations/dance.vrma)
  Original: Universal Animation Library by Quaternius, CC0 1.0
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
  Retargeted from Rigify to VRM humanoid by Ekza Studio (CC0 1.0).
  Kept as the previous dance baseline; no longer used at runtime.

Required credit for the VRoid Project motion pack:
